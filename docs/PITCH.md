# Local 4-minute pitch & global short demo

## Local: 4 minutes + 2 minutes Q&A
- **0:00–0:35** User and concrete problem (Impact)
- **0:35–1:10** Exact selected NASA challenge and relevance (Relevance)
- **1:10–2:30** Working product demonstration; include offline recording fallback (Presentation)
- **2:30–3:10** Real NASA data, technical validation, limitations (Validity)
- **3:10–3:40** Distinctive approach and practical benefit (Creativity/Impact)
- **3:40–4:00** Honest what-works-now statement and next step

Rehearse questions: Where is NASA data really used? What is implemented vs simulated? Why is it better than a simple existing solution? What was validated? What's the biggest limitation?

## Official global demo — separate from local speech
**Choose one:** ≤30-second accessible video, **or** ≤7-slide presentation.
Suggested 7 slides: 1. Team/Challenge; 2. Problem/user; 3. Idea; 4. NASA source/system; 5. Working results; 6. Scientific checks/limitations/impact; 7. Final link and references.

Never present an unbuilt feature as working. Label simulations. Disclose/watermark AI-generated imagery and follow NASA branding rules.

| Criterion (equal weight at Haifa) | Team score 1–5 |
|---|---|
