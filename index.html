04 / SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT

Spaceflight hazards, clearly explained

NASA Human Research Program resources inform which issues are worth monitoring. They do not validate the example measurements or our alert rules.

01

Isolation & confinement

Behavioral well-being and sleep can be affected by demanding, isolated missions. Our voluntary mood and sleep check-ins help surface changes for discussion.

NASA hazards ↗
02

Altered gravity

Long-duration flight can change cardiovascular and musculoskeletal fitness. The prototype monitors resting pulse trends, not bone density or clinical cardiovascular risk.

NASA risks ↗
03

Distance from Earth

Communication delays make clear, independent self-observation useful. The software works locally; clinical decisions remain subject to approved crew procedures.
